Hybrid Health Coaching Landscape
Competitive scan · cardiometabolic coaching

From coaching sessions to a coaching journey

Six programs show how AI and people are already sharing the work of health coaching. The pattern they converge on goes beyond AI covering some sessions: they redesign the whole journey and decide which touchpoints belong to AI and which belong to people.

Redesign the journey

What changes when AI accompanies the whole journey

One way to add AI is to let it deliver some of the sessions. The programs reviewed here mostly do something different: coaches stay at the moments that need a person, and AI stays with the resident in the weeks between, flagging when a coach should step in.

B2B2C

B2B2C

Solves
Body and mind treated together, in tiers of increasing intensity

FlyteHealth

B2B

Market and stage
US only; Series A, about 100 staff

Mainly D2C

B2B

Layer by layer

How the programs handle each layer

LayerWhat the market doesStandout examples
1 Data entry Ships connected devices free, pre-paired and auto-syncing, so data arrives without the member having to report it
  • Omada: scale, BP cuff, glucose meter, CGM by program
  • Hello Heart: FDA-cleared BP cuff syncing silently to the app
  • Vida: labs, pharmacy claims, wearables and 100+ apps
  • Noom: relies on self-logging, which users find time-consuming
2 TriageAI, reviewed by people Software watches each member's patterns and tells people whom to contact, and when
  • Omada: coaches reach out proactively when the system flags a change
  • Hello Heart: AI risk flags (BP spikes, missed doses) trigger self-triage or a pharmacist
  • Vida: members move between Core, Elevate and Control tiers by risk
  • FlyteHealth: AI phenotyping sets treatment intensity
3 Daily supportAI Delivers standard content, interprets data, answers questions, nudges habits
  • Hello Heart Nia: 24/7 assistant that knows the member's history; the LLM handles conversation while guideline-based workflows constrain clinical advice; no diagnosis or dose changes
  • Noom: 5–15 minute daily CBT micro-lessons
  • Omada: weekly interactive lessons
4 Key momentsPeople People own judgment, motivation and prescribing; peers share part of the support load
  • Vida: one care team of physicians, dietitians, therapists and CBT-trained coaches
  • Omada: certified diabetes educators plus peer groups
  • Goodpath: 20+ specialties build each care plan
5 EscalationPeople Defined routes from AI to a clinician, and into the member's own doctor
  • Hello Heart: licensed pharmacists step in on high-risk adherence or drug interactions; the app urges ER visits when needed
  • Omada: shares data with the member's primary care doctor
  • Vida: escalation to obesity specialists in the Elevate tier
Ideas worth borrowing

Four patterns that could shape our design

A passive data entry point

Without one, triage only hears residents who keep talking; the ones quietly dropping off stay invisible.

Two-track safety design (Hello Heart)

An LLM for the conversation, guideline-based workflows for anything clinical.

Explicit care tiers (Vida)

For example: education only, then AI-supported self-management, then coach-led coaching, then clinician review, with clear rules for moving up and down.

Peer support (Omada)

Peer groups that take part of the daily support load.
